🌻 Through the Ups and Downs 🌻

The past year, ever since the last Rakhi, has been full of ups and downs.

But through everything, we somehow stayed strong, and our bond stayed strong too.

At times, I know I can be very difficult to handle.

I behave immaturely in front of you guys, but I think that's only because I feel comfortable around you.

I know I haven't been the same during the past few months.

I have become quieter, more distant and more irritated than before.

But trust me, I have been fighting my own battles, and sometimes I don't know how to deal with them.

Lately, I know I have become a little selfish.

I overthink things a lot, and sometimes I make things much more complicated than they need to be.

I know I can be annoying, but I hope you know that none of it ever comes from a bad place.

❤️ Always ❤️

Maybe that is why writing something for this year's Rakhi feels so difficult.

Last year, it didn't even take a day. I simply wrote whatever was in my heart.

Maybe our lives are beginning to move in different directions now.

The day I realised that you were really leaving Genpact, my heart skipped a beat.

I didn't know what to do.

I didn't know how to move forward.

Even now, the office doesn't feel like an office without you.

Maybe that is why I have been working from home so much lately.

I don't even feel like going to the office anymore.

I know you will meet wonderful people in your life and create countless new memories.

But keep a tiny little space for me somewhere in that world as well.

Because no matter what happens, you will always have a place in my heart.

Always.

— Adiluuu ❤️
